To test out the AIs:
- Load Unity (version 6000.6.0f1) and in the file inspector, go to "Assets" -> "Scenes" -> and double-click on the Scene asset called "EnemyTesting"
- Press play to begin testing
- Use WSAD to navigate, use shift to sprint. Pressing L. Click fires a few particles (we didn't focus on setting up actual combat), and holding R. Click puts the player into an aiming mode.

Enemy Colors (these were added AFTER most videos were recorded)
- Orange: Beast
- Purple: Drunk
- Red: Brute
- Green: Knife/Fast
- Blue: Shooter

They're all spread out around the map.

Enjoy!
